## Next: village raids (design)
- Unlock: 12+ plots owned. Rare: at most one raid per ~40 min of play, never in the first 10 min of
  a session, never while a fight or menu is open; more likely in Winter Storms.
- A warning bell and a banner 20 s ahead; a monster surfaces off the nearest shore and crawls or
  lunges at the nearest building. Raid ends when its health is gone or after 90 s.
- Defence: (a) harbour cannons, a new building you place freely, auto-fires at monsters in range,
  3 levels; (b) hand weapons from the shop (harpoon gun, boarding axe, flare pistol): tap the
  monster to throw/shoot, with a cooldown; (c) the crew helps if a Crew Hall or better stands.
- Stakes: a hit building stops earning until repaired (coins), never destroyed. Driving the
  monster off pays a bounty, pearls and a trophy; killing it lands it as a catch.
- Needs: cannon models x3, 3 weapon models, monster land/attack animation, hit VFX, raid HUD
  (monster health bar, building health), economy entries, save fields. Code first with the three
  existing monsters; models when the Blender agent is back.
